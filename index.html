HOW TO PLAY

Take turns placing X and O. Connect three across, down, or diagonally to win. A full board without a winner is a draw. Marks stay on the board until the next round.

In solo play choose X or O. X opens the first round. Round starters alternate; the CPU may open the next round. Easy plays randomly, Medium wins or blocks when possible, and Hard plans ahead.

Use a mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter. Arrow keys move between board squares. First to 3 wins ends when either player earns three wins; draws do not count toward the target. Scores last until you reset, change game setup, or reload the page. Your preferences are remembered when browser storage is available. Shell and sound changes keep your scores.

Opening setup pauses play; close it or press RESUME to continue unchanged. HINT outlines a strong move in solo play. UNDO is available only in endless solo play and takes back your last move and the CPU reply. TITLE clears the session after confirmation during an unfinished round.
